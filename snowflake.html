FAQ

Snowflake + DreamFactory FAQ

How do I install DreamFactory from Snowflake Marketplace?

Search for 'DreamFactory' in the Snowflake Marketplace, click 'Get', and follow the setup wizard. The Native App deploys within your Snowflake account with no external infrastructure needed.

Does DreamFactory use my Snowflake compute credits?

Yes. DreamFactory queries run on your designated Snowflake warehouse. You control compute size, auto-suspend, and auto-resume — exactly like any other Snowflake workload.

Can I use DreamFactory with Snowflake Data Shares?

Yes. DreamFactory can generate REST APIs from shared databases and data products in your Snowflake account, making shared data accessible to downstream applications.

Is DreamFactory available on all Snowflake clouds?

Yes. DreamFactory works with Snowflake on AWS, Azure, and GCP across all supported regions.
