FAQ

Pricing Questions

How is pricing calculated for the Enterprise tier?

Enterprise pricing is based on the number of vCPUs allocated to your DreamFactory deployment. Contact our sales team for a custom quote based on your infrastructure.

Can I start with Open Source and upgrade later?

Yes. The open-source edition uses the same codebase as commercial editions. Upgrading is a license key change — no reinstallation or data migration required.

What databases are excluded from the Professional tier?

The Professional tier includes all connectors except Oracle, Snowflake, and IBM DB2. These are available in the Enterprise tier.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes. All listed prices reflect annual billing. Monthly billing is available at a higher rate. Contact sales for details.

Need help choosing?

Talk to our engineering team. We'll help you pick the right plan for your databases, deployment model, and team size.
